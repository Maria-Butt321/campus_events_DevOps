About Campus Events

Who We Are

Campus Events is a student-driven initiative that helps organize, promote, and manage all academic and recreational activities happening across the university.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make event management easier for students and faculty by providing a central platform where they can view, plan, and participate in events.

What We Do

  • Manage event schedules
  • Provide event announcements
  • Share galleries and highlights
  • Help students stay updated
